<h1 align="center">Clepsydra & Path4Med Projects</h1>

<p align="center">
  This repository tracks the development and progress of the <strong>Clepsydra</strong> and <strong>Path4Med</strong> projects, conducted at the <em>Instituto Superior de Agronomia (ISA)</em>.
  These initiatives focus on innovative approaches to sustainable water management, precision agriculture, and the integration of data-driven tools such as machine learning and geospatial analysis.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Both projects aim to support climate-resilient farming practices, enhance water quality monitoring, and promote efficient use of resources in vulnerable Mediterranean agro-hydrosystems.
</p>
